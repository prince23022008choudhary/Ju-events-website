A LITTLE HELP GOES A LONG WAY

Let's get you to your next event.

Everything you need to discover, register, and show up.

Your questions, answered.

How do I register for an event?

Create a campus account, confirm your email, and sign in. Open an event and select Register now. Enter your full name and student ID to reserve a place. Your registration appears in My registrations.

Where is my ticket?

Sign in and select My registrations in the sidebar. You can view your personal reference and download an event calendar file. Bring your student ID and registration reference to the event.

Can I cancel my registration?

Yes. Open My registrations and select Cancel registration on your ticket. You can cancel before the event starts, and your place becomes available to another student.

I didn't receive a confirmation email.

Check your spam folder first. Try signing up again using the same email to resend confirmation. For password issues, use Forgot password in the sign-in dialog. Your account must have a verified email to register.

Are these event listings official?

The launch catalog contains illustrative sample events, visibly marked Preview. Confirm any published event details with its organizer. Sample registrations are for exploring the portal, not admission to an actual university event.

What does registration cost?

The current portal supports free registrations only. It does not collect payments or financial information. Never send payment details through this site.

Good to know before you go.

All event times use India Standard Time (Asia/Kolkata). Bring a valid student ID to campus activities and follow the organizer's event guidelines.

If a listing needs clarification, contact the organizing club through the university's established campus channels. Don't share passwords or personal ticket references publicly.

This portal does not verify university enrollment automatically. Your email account is verified, while student IDs are self-reported and should be checked by event organizers.
